# SketchLive: real demo shot list

Target: 6–10 seconds, under 8 MB, about 800 px wide, 12–15 fps. Capture the real app with ScreenToGif; save as `media/demo.gif`.

1. Preload SD-Turbo on your GPU, enter a short prompt and start with a blank real canvas.
2. Draw two or three clear shapes while the real preview follows your strokes.
3. Change a style or press Enhance to reveal the finished result.
4. Hold sketch and output side by side. Record real inference; never use --mock.

Hold the final result for 2–3 seconds. Disclose speedups/time cuts. Do not create UI mockups or present simulated output as real inference.
